18Mon
Atal Tunnel → Sissu → Vashisht
~80 km round trip · depends on snow and police
SnowCheck first
▸
Check the evening before and that morning
  • Sissu tourism closure: every winter the Sissu village council shuts tourism for the Halda/Losar festivals (2024: 15 Jan–28 Feb; 2026: 20 Jan–28 Feb). The 2027 dates usually come out in early to mid January. 18 Jan is right at the edge.
  • Road past Solang: after fresh snow, police stop tourist vehicles at Solang Nala, sometimes for days (Jan 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2026).
  • Before
    Book a Manali union 4x4 (Scorpio) with snow chains, ~₹3,000–6,000 per vehicle. After snow only 4x4s are let through. The driver usually handles the e-Aagman vehicle registration (eaagman.hp.gov.in) and the small entry taxes
  • 9:00
    Leave Manali → Solang Nala barrier (~14 km)
  • ~10:00
    In past winters vehicles were let past Solang only from ~10 AM and had to return through the tunnel by 3–4 PM
  • Late morning
    Atal Tunnel (~9 km, no toll, no stopping inside) → Sissu (~40 km from Manali)
    • Sissu viewpoint, Chandra River, partly frozen waterfall
    • Snow photography, village walk
    • Tea and Maggi at roadside stalls (may be shut if the closure is on)
    • Park where police direct you
  • 2:00–2:30
    Head back through the tunnel
  • 3:30–4:00
    Back in Manali (sunset ~5:40 PM, black ice after dark)
  • 4:00–5:30
    ♨️ Vashisht Temple + hot springs (~3 km from Mall Road)

How the day can go

  • Road open, Sissu open: plan as above
  • Road open, Sissu closed: ask police at Solang about going to the tunnel's far end for photos, otherwise a second Solang visit
  • Road closed past Solang: Solang again or a rest morning, then Vashisht + the Jogini Falls walk
  • Heavy snow falling: don't go past Solang even if allowed
Weather: Sissu ~−2°C to 5°C by day, −8°C to −15°C at night · 🛏️ Night: Manali

Getting around

Manali (Days 3–4)

  • Solang round trip: Manali taxi-union SUV (Innova/Scorpio, fits 6), ~₹2,000–4,500 per vehicle
  • Sissu: union 4x4 with snow chains, ~₹3,000–6,000 per vehicle; book the evening before
  • The HRTC Manali → Keylong bus passes Sissu, but winter services are few and the return is unreliable
  • Hadimba, Old Manali, Vashisht: walk or autos

Manali → Kasol (Day 5) Bus

  • Frequent local buses Manali → Kullu, then Kullu/Bhuntar → Kasol (buses towards Manikaran)
  • Kasol ↔ Manikaran: Bhuntar–Manikaran local buses (~6 AM–6:30 PM) or a shared taxi

Kasol → Bir (Day 6) Taxi

  • One-way Innova Crysta / Ertiga, ~₹4,000–6,000 per vehicle
  • Bus alternative: Kasol → Bhuntar → HRTC towards Baijnath/Palampur → Bir turn-off → local taxi (~6–7 hrs)

Bir → Barot (Day 7) Bus

  • Bus from Bir (Chowgan Chowk) to Jogindernagar or Barot. Shared jeeps from Ghatasani every 45–60 min till ~5 PM
  • Buses to and from Barot thin out after ~4 PM in winter

Barot → Amritsar (Day 8) Taxi

  • One-way Ertiga / Innova, ~₹4,000–6,000 per vehicle
  • Bus only works if you leave Barot by ~7 AM (Jogindernagar → HRTC to Amritsar)

Luggage for 6 is tight in an Innova or Ertiga: ask for a roof carrier, or take a Tempo Traveller.

Tips

  • 🧥 Layers, gloves, woollen cap, sunglasses for the snow glare. Rent snow boots in Manali or Solang
  • 💵 Carry cash: ATMs around Solang and Sissu are unreliable; local buses take cash only
  • 🌙 Finish every hill drive before dark (~5:30 PM). Roads ice over after sunset
  • ❄️ Check road and snow status the evening before every hill day
  • 📏 Distances and fares are approximate (travel and cab sites often disagree). Confirm on Google Maps and with operators
