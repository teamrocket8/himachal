Mon, 25 JanSNOWCHECK FIRST▸

Atal Tunnel → Sissu → Vashisht hot springs

~80 km round trip, depends on snow + police

check the night before:

1. Sissu shuts to tourists every winter for local festivals (2024: from 15 Jan, 2026: from 20 Jan). 2027 dates come out in early jan. the 25th falls inside that window every recent year, so expect Sissu to be closed.

2. after fresh snow, police stop cars at Solang. happened most januaries.

  • Book a local 4x4 (Scorpio) with snow chains, ~₹3,000–6,000. driver sorts the e-Aagman pass + entry taxnight before
  • Leave Manali9 am
  • Solang barrier. in past winters they let cars through only from ~10 am~10 am
  • Atal Tunnel (9 km) → Sissu: viewpoint, Chandra river, frozen-ish waterfall, maggilate morning
  • Head back through the tunnel (they wanted people back by 3–4 pm)2 – 2:30
  • Back in Manali3:30 – 4
  • Vashisht temple + hot springs. best way to end a cold day4 – 5:30

if Sissu's closed → ask police at Solang if we can at least go to the tunnel's far end, else Solang round 2

if road's closed → Solang again or sleep in, then Vashisht + Jogini Falls walk

if it's snowing hard → don't go past Solang, people have been stuck there for hours

Atal Tunnel

~25 km from Manali · 9 km long

  • just a drive through the mountain, no toll, no permit
  • no stopping inside the tunnel
  • after the tunnel, park where police tell us (e.g. Sissu helipad area)

Sissu

~40 km from Manali · ~1.5–2 hrs here

  • Sissu viewpoint, snowy mountains all around
  • Chandra river, partly frozen
  • Sissu waterfall, partly frozen in deep winter
  • snow photography
  • tea / maggi at the dhabas on the main road (might be shut if the closure's on)
  • walk around the village

Vashisht

~3 km from Mall Road · evening

  • Vashisht temple
  • natural hot springs, perfect after the cold

sleeping: Manali · Sissu: −2 to 5°C by day, −8 to −15°C at night. layers, gloves, cap, sunglasses

Rafting at Pirdi?

~3–4 km past Kullu · on the way to Bhuntar · maybe

  • Beas rafting season is Mar–Jun & Sep–Nov
  • late Jan = freezing water, most likely shut
  • ask at the counters on the day, only go licensed
  • if it's on, ~1 hr add-on. don't plan around it
